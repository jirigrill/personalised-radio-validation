# 13 — Which concept variant survives the constraints?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 05, 06, 07, 08, 09

## Question

A decision ticket, worked live with the developer. By this point the constraints are known: which
music source survives (05), what podcast and news audio is usable (06), what the platforms and indie
apps already ship (07, 08), and what evidence of demand exists (09).

Decide **which single concept variant, if any, is worth costing** — the input to the final verdict.

Bring to the conversation:

- The surviving music source and what it costs the concept (from 05).
- Whether the **news layer** survives at all, or should be cut from the MVP (06).
- The specific **unoccupied gap**, if 07 and 08 found one — and whether that gap is a *product* gap
  or a *nobody-wants-this* signal. The prior project's hardest lesson: an empty space is more often
  a graveyard than an opportunity.
- The honest **incremental value** over the status quo of a playlist plus a podcast app (09).

Questions to resolve with the developer:

1. If Spotify DJ already does AI commentary over personalised music for free, what is left that a
   user would pay for? Name it as one sentence, not a feature list.
2. Which of the five concept steps are **load-bearing** and which are decoration? If the AI intros
   are cut (10) and news is cut (06), is "podcast interleaved with your liked songs" still a product?
3. Is the developer's own commute the **actual** target use case, and how many people share it
   closely enough to pay? Being in the segment is an advantage; being the only member of it is not.
4. What is their **alternative use of this time**? A "don't build" verdict is meaningless without it,
   and the real question may be "is this the best of my ideas" rather than "is this viable".

Output: one named variant to cost, or the conclusion that no variant clears the bar — in which case
the arithmetic becomes moot and the map short-circuits to the verdict.
